# Pattern: Estimate vs Target vs Commitment

**Status:** Active  
**Origin:** SI Solution Architect training (Program Line N)

## Pattern statement

Three different sentences get treated as one number. Separate them or the estimate becomes a political object.

| Term | What it is |
|------|------------|
| **Estimate** | Tentative evaluation of effort/cost given a defined scope and risk — a **judgment**, not a wish |
| **Target** | A desirable business objective (“ready for the show in May”; “cap at this budget”) |
| **Commitment** | A **promise** to deliver defined functionality at a stated quality by a date |

Pricing is a fourth conversation. **The work is the work:** do not let price rewrite effort, approach, or the resource plan. Then make estimate, commercial model, SOW, and Solution Plan **tick and tie**.

## Tests

- Can we name the target without calling it the estimate?  
- Is the commitment dated, scoped, and quality-bounded — or only a budget?  
- If sales needs a lower price, did we change **scope/risk/approach**, or only the hours column?
