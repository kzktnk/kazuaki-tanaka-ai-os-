# Pattern: Operational Reality

**Status:** Active  
**Origin:** LinkedIn SP08

## Pattern statement

Power and OT digital transformation fails when it ignores Operational Reality—the messy, exception-driven, legacy-constrained environment where operations actually run.

## Core distinction

### Idealized Operations

Standardized processes, clean data, documented configurations, and linear workflows assumed by many DX/AI roadmaps.

### Operational Reality

Legacy systems, local optimizations, tacit knowledge, exception handling, and provisional workarounds that keep critical systems running.

## Signals

Use this pattern when:

- AI initiatives assume clean data and standardized processes that do not exist
- field teams bypass "official" workflows with tacit practices
- DX programs focus on AI/IoT before EAM, CMDB, or document foundations
- transformation plans deny or erase on-the-ground rationality

## Design response

1. **Do not deny Operational Reality** — field practices often contain valid constraints
2. **Make tacit knowledge visible** — document exceptions, workarounds, and context before automating
3. **Build foundations first** — EAM, document management, CMDB, process standardization, data readiness
4. **Connect AI to reality** — integrate with how operations actually work, not how slides describe them

## Core rule

> AI needs understandable structure to perform. Operational Reality is where that structure must be built—or honestly mapped—before scale is possible.
