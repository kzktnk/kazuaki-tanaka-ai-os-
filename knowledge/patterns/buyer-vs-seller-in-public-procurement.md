# Pattern: Buyer vs Seller in Public Procurement

**Status:** Active  
**Origin:** Public / defense IT engagements (Program Line P)

## Pattern statement

公共調達では、コンサルタントは **官側の公平** か **応札者の勝ち** のどちらかに立つ。同じプログラムサイクルで両方の記憶を使うな。成果物の型が違う。

| Stance | Success | Forbidden |
|--------|---------|-----------|
| **Buyer support** | Defensible spec, evaluation, scrutiny; protest-resistant file | Coaching one vendor; leaking baseline to a bidder |
| **Seller bid** | Compliant, deliverable, priced offer mapped to evaluation items | Using another vendor’s hearing notes; rewriting the official’s risk decision as yours |

## Tests

- If this document leaked, would a losing vendor have grounds to claim coaching?  
- Are we writing a **仕様／基準／精査報告**, or a **提案のキーメッセージ**?  
- Is authorization / risk acceptance still the official’s, with us only advising?
