## Core Theme

AI should compress effort that no longer needs human time, without removing the exposure through which junior judgment is formed. Reviewing a finished AI answer is not a substitute for that exposure. No.25 is the work-design response to the Development Rationale in No.24, held together with the Authority Rationale in No.23.

## Key Distinctions

| Common framing | Reframed insight |
|----------------|------------------|
| Keep juniors on tasks AI can do | Preserves effort; wastes the gain |
| Have juniors review AI output | Reviewing well needs judgment that review alone does not build |
| Effort saved = experience gained | Effort and exposure are different |
| Preserve every old task | Some old work was waste; AI can also create new exposure |
| Time saved and output quality | Also ask whether people can make harder decisions over time |

## Five-step sequence

```text
Raw contact → AI organizes → Independent judgment → Compare → Retrospect
```

Raw contact comes before any AI summary. Independent judgment comes before AI's recommendation.

## Relationship to No.23–24

No.23: **who decides** (Authority). Capability does not grant permission.

No.24: **who learns by doing** (Development). Doing the work is how judgment is formed.

No.25: **how the work is designed** so both can survive AI adoption. Compress effort; keep exposure.
