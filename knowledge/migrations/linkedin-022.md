## Primary contribution

Standards should be compressed organizational learning, not a layer separate from experience. Recurring exceptions with consistent reasoning and better outcomes are evidence about the standard. Memory becomes learning in the loop Exception → reasoning → outcome → pattern → standard. AI makes the loop visible; it must not silently rewrite the standard.
