# Lesson: Dual Roadmap Messaging

**Version:** v1.0  
**Status:** Active  
**Origin:** Executive workshop practice — internal maturity design language vs client-safe narrative.

---

## Lesson

Internal AI roadmaps often combine **Role×Level grids** and **year-specific task tables**. These are design tools, not slide content. Executives and external stakeholders need **phase narrative** and **outcome-linked stories**.

Technology-only roadmaps sound like tool procurement. People-only roadmaps sound like training plans. **Pair them in conversation**, but **show only the simplified layer** externally.

---

## Executive-safe vs internal-only

| Internal (do not show) | Executive / client phrasing |
|------------------------|----------------------------|
| User Lv2 @ Year 2 | Year 2: production use cases; daily AI use in the field |
| Manager Lv3 @ Year 3 | Year 3: embed AI in workflows; final decisions stay human |
| Developer Lv2 RAG | Year 2: search, summarization, similar-case support |
| Governor Lv2 | Year 2: usage review, data access, approval paths |
| Initiatives ①②③ (numbered client deck) | Business impact / knowledge transfer / adoption foundation |
| Five-role level table | Role-based maturity (details in program design) |

---

## Core phrases (plain language)

| Concept | Say |
|---------|-----|
| Authority Design | AI permission design — suggest first, automate later |
| Expertise Amplification | Strengthen expert judgment; don't replace experts |
| Operational Reality | Design for how the field actually works |
| Capability ≠ Authority | Can do ≠ allowed to do |
| Enabler vs outcome KPI | Separate means metrics from business results |
