## Purpose

Capture **how a transformation consultant should relate to PMO work** — not only what PMO does, but the professional stance that makes PMO credible to executives and delivery teams.

Use when:

- Positioning PMO in a proposal or steering narrative
- Coaching internal PMO leaders
- Aligning AI-generated recommendations with Tanaka’s consulting voice

---

## Value Proposition (Training Goal)

A practitioner should be able to explain:

1. **What PMO is** — program-level governance for strategy realization
2. **Why it exists** — projects alone do not execute strategy
3. **What PMO delivers** — integration, portfolio discipline, change leadership, benefit traceability

**PMO is a means.** The end is **Transformation** — durable change in what the organization does and how it operates.

Approach enterprise transformation from **Point of View (PoV)** through delivery; PMO is how execution stays aligned after the strategy story is told.

---

## Five Principles of a Professional Transformation Contractor

Paraphrased from LPD closing principles; wording generalized.

### 1. Practice first

Do not debate “can we?” indefinitely — **practice with pmo or PMO** in real contexts. Solutions evolve through practice, not slides alone.

### 2. Strategy without execution is not strategy

Be a strategist **and** a PM professional. **A strategy that is not executed is not a strategy** — it is a document.

### 3. Create and share knowledge

Do not only consume playbooks. **Develop, adapt, and share** PMO solutions for your context. Knowledge received must become knowledge owned.

### 4. Teach to deepen understanding

Participating in LPD-style learning is not enough — **facilitate others**. Teaching clarifies gaps in your own model.

### 5. Understand before you sell

You cannot credibly sell what you do not understand. Deep immersion precedes client-facing offering.

---

## Implications for Deliverables

| Principle | Deliverable behavior |
|-----------|---------------------|
| Practice first | Prefer pilot program / wave over big-bang PMO blueprint |
| Execution = strategy | Tie every PMO design choice to a strategic outcome or benefit |
| Create knowledge | Document client-specific RACI, portfolio rules, lessons learned |
| Teach | Executive briefings that transfer the model, not only status |
| Understand first | PoV and gap analysis before PMO org chart |
