# Lesson: Frame AI Governance Around Accountability

**Status:** Active  
**Origin:** LinkedIn No.13 and No.14

## Observation

AI governance is often communicated as a mechanism for limiting AI, preventing errors, or requiring approvals.

This framing can make governance appear defensive or obstructive.

## Lesson

For executive and operational audiences, governance is more compelling when framed around:

- decision ownership
- accountability
- authority
- intervention
- explainability
- acceptance of consequences

## Why it works

The accountability framing moves the discussion from:

> Can AI do this?

to:

> Who owns the consequences?

## Communication guidance

Prefer questions such as:

- Who reviewed the recommendation?
- Who approved the action?
- Who can override it?
- Who owns the outcome?
- Who explains the decision?

Avoid presenting human oversight only as evidence that AI is not yet capable.
