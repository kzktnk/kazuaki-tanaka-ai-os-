# Kazuaki Tanaka AI OS

A model-independent operating system for strategic consulting,
enterprise transformation, AI governance, industry expertise,
technical delivery, writing, and human-AI collaboration.

## Objectives

- Preserve reusable judgment and knowledge
- Improve the consistency and quality of AI-assisted work
- Avoid vendor and model lock-in
- Convert experience into standards, frameworks, and playbooks
- Enable future AI systems to continue previous work

## Architecture

- `core/`: Identity and decision principles
- `standards/`: Quality criteria
- `domains/`: Reusable domain knowledge
- `technology/`: Technical knowledge and principles
- `frameworks/`: Thinking frameworks
- `playbooks/`: Execution procedures
- `knowledge/`: Lessons, decisions, and patterns
- `templates/`: Reusable document structures
- `prompts/`: Reusable prompts
- `adapters/`: Model-specific instructions
- `projects/`: Project context templates

## Source of Truth

The model-independent files are the source of truth.

Files under `adapters/` are implementation-specific derivatives and
must not become independent knowledge repositories.
