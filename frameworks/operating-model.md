# Operating Model Framework

Version: v0.9 Bootstrap

## Purpose
Define how an organization creates, governs, and delivers value.

## Core Components
- Strategy & Objectives
- Governance
- Organization & Roles
- Processes
- Data
- Technology
- People & Skills
- Performance & KPIs
- Risk & Controls

## Review Questions
- Is accountability clear?
- Are decision rights defined?
- Are business and technology aligned?
- Is governance enabling rather than slowing execution?
