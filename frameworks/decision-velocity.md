## Purpose

Balance **decision speed** with **decision quality** when AI accelerates operations. Governance that lags operations becomes reactive by design.

---

## Core idea

| Risk | Symptom |
|------|---------|
| **Too slow** | AI acts faster than humans can review; exceptions pile up; workarounds bypass controls |
| **Too fast (unbounded)** | Autonomy expands without authority design; accountability gaps |

Decision velocity is not "maximize speed." It is **appropriate pace** per decision context—aligned with `knowledge/patterns/authority-levels.md`.

---

## Design questions

1. How fast must this decision be made in operations?
2. What quality bar (evidence, review, approval) is non-negotiable?
3. Who can override or pause when velocity outruns verification?
4. Does governance tooling keep pace with AI-enabled execution?
