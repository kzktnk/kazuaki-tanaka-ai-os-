# Transformation Roadmap Framework

**Version:** v1.0  
**Status:** Active  
**Owner:** Kazuaki Tanaka  

---

## Purpose

Plan transformation as a sequence of measurable capability improvements.

For **AI-specific dual-layer roadmaps** (technology × people, Year 1–5), see `frameworks/ai-adoption-roadmap.md`.

---

## Phases

1. Current State Assessment  
2. Vision & Target State  
3. Gap Analysis  
4. Prioritization  
5. Implementation  
6. Adoption  
7. Continuous Improvement  

---

## Mapping — Seven Phases × AI Year Narrative

Illustrative overlay when AI is a major enabler (`ai-adoption-roadmap.md`):

| Seven-phase | AI Year (example) |
|-------------|-------------------|
| Assessment / Vision / Gap | Year 1 foundation |
| Prioritization / Implementation | Year 2 field impact |
| Adoption | Year 3 business integration |
| Continuous Improvement | Year 4–5 scale and co-work |

Use `frameworks/thinking-patterns/pattern-04-plan-build-run-improve.md` for Plan → Build → Run → Improve cycling within each horizon.

---

## Success Factors

- Executive sponsorship  
- Business ownership  
- Change management  
- KPI tracking  
- Feedback loops  
- Program-level governance (see `frameworks/transformation-pmo.md`)
