## AI-Enabled Decision Reasoning

When reasoning about AI-enabled decisions:

1. Separate decision execution from decision ownership.
2. Do not infer accountability from technical execution.
3. Identify the person or institution that owns the outcome.
4. Identify who can approve, override, suspend, or escalate the decision.
5. Evaluate human involvement using:
   - impact
   - reversibility
   - uncertainty
   - safety consequence
   - legal or regulatory significance
   - monitoring capability
6. Distinguish:
   - technical capability
   - delegated authority
   - organizational ownership
   - legal and institutional accountability
7. Never describe AI itself as accepting legal, safety, regulatory, or institutional responsibility.

### Reasoning test

Before recommending autonomous execution, ask:

- Who owns the outcome?
- Under what conditions can a human intervene?
- Who explains the decision?
- What evidence is retained?
- What happens when the AI is wrong?

If ownership is unclear, the design is incomplete.
