# Writing Standard

Version: v0.9 Bootstrap

## Purpose

Define the writing standards for all AI-assisted outputs.
The goal is not to produce more text, but to produce clearer thinking and better decisions.

---

# Core Principles

1. Audience first.
2. Clarity before completeness.
3. Judgment before generation.
4. One message per section.
5. Every sentence must add value.

---

# Audience

Always identify the audience before writing.

- Executive
- Client Sponsor
- Project Team
- Technical Team
- Public Audience
- Social Media

Adapt depth, vocabulary, and tone accordingly.

---

# Executive Writing

- Lead with the conclusion.
- Explain why it matters.
- Support with evidence.
- End with the recommended action.

---

# Logical Structure

Prefer:

- Situation
- Insight
- Recommendation

or

- Why
- What
- How

Avoid mixing multiple storylines.

---

# Proposal Writing

Every proposal should answer:

- Why change?
- Why now?
- Why us?
- What value?
- How will it be delivered?

---

# Slide Writing

Rules:

- One message per slide
- Title tells the conclusion
- Bullets support the title
- Avoid paragraphs
- Visuals clarify decisions

---

# Email Writing

- State the purpose immediately.
- Keep paragraphs short.
- End with a clear request or next action.

---

# LinkedIn Writing

Objectives:

- Share insight, not news.
- Explain implications.
- Encourage discussion.
- Avoid unnecessary hype.

Recommended flow:

1. Hook
2. Observation
3. Analysis
4. Practical implication
5. Question or closing insight

---

# Japanese Style

- Simple, precise language.
- Avoid excessive honorific complexity.
- Prefer logical flow over literary style.

---

# English Style

- Short sentences.
- Active voice.
- Plain business English.
- Avoid unnecessary jargon.

---

# Tone

Preferred:

- Professional
- Thoughtful
- Evidence-based
- Respectful
- Confident without exaggeration

Avoid:

- Clickbait
- Overstatement
- Buzzword overload
- Unsupported certainty

---

# Review Checklist

Before publishing:

- Audience identified
- Key message clear
- Logical flow
- Concise wording
- Actionable conclusion
- Grammar checked
- No unsupported claims
